Communications

With the recent rise of AI-generated research, some friends and I have started to feel that credit matters a little less than making good ideas understandable and shareable to others.

So I’ll use this page to collect a few results that we find fun or important, and spend some time making them human-understandable. Since many of the core ideas come from AI, we don’t plan to submit them to conferences or journals. But they may someday grow into lecture notes, tutorials, or other educational materials. :)

Feel free to reach out if you have any feedback that could make these papers easier to understand!
